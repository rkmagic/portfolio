# Portfolio

A personal product portfolio: case studies, product teardowns, and writing on consumer mobile and AI-native work.

## What’s here

- **Home** — short intro, featured work, then browsing by projects, writings, and teardowns
- **Projects** — shipped builds and case studies (context, trade-offs, outcomes)
- **Writings** — articles and PDF decks from workshops and coursework
- **Teardowns** — structured critiques of real products
- **About** — background, focus areas, and highlights

Content is MDX under `content/` (`projects/`, `writings/`, `teardowns/`). Static assets (images, PDFs, resume) live in `public/`.
