## Context

Date: 30 October, 2025

I had a course in my MBA around innovation management. We had a mini project to research and predict
the future of a technology using innovation frameworks and the challenges that it has before scaling.

My group was given autonomous AI agents as our topic. I was leading the project.

## Key points

- Autonomous AI agents were the buzz but adoption was yet to cross the chasm to early majority.
- Data Privacy, Trust, Legacy Systems and People Skills were top challenges to be solved.
