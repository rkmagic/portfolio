## Context

February, 2026

Part of the MBA, we had a course on data fleuncy and our team chose to analyze life expectancy data.

The goal was to find drivers of life expectancy and analyze patterns across countries split by different parameters like GDP/capita and schooling.

Dataset was from 2000 to 2015 for 193 countries.

## Key points

- High HDI and population didn't correlate with life expectancy. "Resource Curse" impacted countries with High HDI like Angola and Cameroon.
- Schooling showed a high correlation with life expectancy. Each additional year of schooling was associated with ~2-year increase in life expectancy.
- Adult and child mortality has reduced significantly but was still prevalent in countries affected by conflict and instability.
- Data didn't capture information after 2015 so next generation problems like air pollution needs to be tackled.
